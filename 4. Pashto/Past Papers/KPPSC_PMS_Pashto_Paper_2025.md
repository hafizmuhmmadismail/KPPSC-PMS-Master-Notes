### سوال نمبر 1 (لازمي) — [20 نمرې]

#### (الف) Translate the following passage into literary Pashto: [14 نمرې]
> "A Clean and Green Pakistan is not merely an aesthetic ambition-it is the lifeline of our nation's future. The land that once echoed with the fragrance of orange blossoms and jasmine now struggles under the weight of urban smog and deforestation. As the adage goes, “We do not inherit the earth from our ancestors; we borrow it from our children.” If we continue to treat our soil as a dumping ground and our rivers as sewers, we will sow the wind and reap the whirlwind. The road to a verdant Pakistan lies in grassroots action-community gardens in barren plots, rooftop plantations in crowded cities, and eco-friendly alternatives replacing harmful plastics. When citizens become custodians of their environment, cleanliness transforms from a campaign into a culture, and Pakistan can blossom into the emerald heart of South Asia once more."
