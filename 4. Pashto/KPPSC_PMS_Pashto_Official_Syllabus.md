# KPPSC PMS — Pashto (Optional) Official Syllabus & Examination Architecture
> **Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Public Service Commission (KPPSC) — Provincial Management Service (PMS)**  
> **Subject Code:** Pashto (Optional) | **Total Marks:** 100 | **Exam Duration:** 03 Hours | **Language of Answering:** Pashto (پښتو)

---

## 🏛️ Examination Blueprint & Weightage Structure

| Section / Component | Scope & Content | Typical Marks | Question Type |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **Section 1: Bi-directional Translation & Proverbs** | • English to Pashto prose translation (Current / Social / Ecological themes)<br>• Idiomatic Proverbs (متلونه) Translation (English/Urdu to Pashto)<br>• Pashto to English prose translation | **40 Marks** | **Compulsory** (Q.1 & Q.2) |
| **Section 2: Classical & Sufi Literature** | • Khushal Khan Khattak (Nationalism, Bazm-o-Razm, Dastar-Nama)<br>• Rahman Baba (Sufism, Humanism, Lyricism, Aesthetic simplicity)<br>• Abdul Hamid Baba (Sabk-e-Hindi / Indian Style, Complex Metaphors, Nazuk-Khayali) | **20 Marks** | Choice (Attempt from Q.3–Q.7) |
| **Section 3: Folk Literature (اولسي ادب / فولکلور)** | • Origins, cultural preservation, and characteristics of Pashtun folklore<br>• Core poetic folk genres: Tappa (ټپه), Charbaita (چاربيته), Badala (بدله), Neemakai (نيمکۍ), Lobha (لوبه)<br>• Folk narratives & cultural proverbs (متلونه او اولسي قېصې) | **20 Marks** | Choice (Attempt from Q.3–Q.7) |
| **Section 4: Modern Genres & Prose Evolution** | • Evolution of Pashto Drama (Stage, Radio, TV, and Literary Drama)<br>• Pashto Novel and Short Story (افسانه نګاري)<br>• Critical movements (د ترقۍ پسند مصنفينو تحریک، ادبي جرګې) | **20 Marks** | Choice (Attempt from Q.3–Q.7) |
| **Section 5: Modern Poets & Contemporary Voices** | • Amir Hamza Khan Shinwari (د پښتو غزل بابا)<br>• Ghani Khan (Philosophical, romantic, rebellious imagery)<br>• Contemporary progressive voices: Salim Raz, Dr. Azam Azam, Rahmat Shah Sayel | **20 Marks** | Choice (Attempt from Q.3–Q.7) |

---

## 📋 Standard Paper Scheme (Rubric Analysis)
- **Question 1 (Compulsory — 20 Marks)**:
  - (A) English to Pashto passage translation (14 Marks)
  - (B) English Proverbs to authentic Pashto proverbs (6 Marks)
- **Question 2 (Compulsory — 20 Marks)**:
  - (A) Pashto to English literary translation (20 Marks)
- **Questions 3 to 7 (Choice: Attempt Any Three — 20 Marks Each = 60 Marks)**:
  - Theoretical, analytical, and critical essays on Pashto poets, literary movements, folk literature, and modern genres.
